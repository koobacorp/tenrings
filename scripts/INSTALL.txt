TENRINGS v1.2 - camera hand instrument (VST3, Mac, Apple Silicon + Intel)
=======================================================================

1) INSTALL
   Double-click install_mac.sh (or run it in Terminal), OR copy TENRINGS.vst3 to:
       ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins (or "Find more plugins").
   TENRINGS shows up as an instrument (Generator) by koobacorp. Add it to the Channel Rack.

2) CAMERA PERMISSION
   The first time the camera turns on, macOS asks if FL Studio may use the camera - click Allow.
   If you clicked Don't Allow: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera > turn on FL Studio,
   then restart FL. The status line at the bottom of TENRINGS tells you what is wrong.
   If it says the host has no camera permission entry, macOS is not letting FL itself use any
   camera - tell me and we will switch to the backup plan. Pads and MIDI still work meanwhile.
   Tip: good light, both hands in frame. An iPhone as Continuity Camera often gives 60 fps.

3) PLAY
   Mode buttons: DRUM (orange), KEYBOARD (turquoise), LEAD (purple), STRINGS (green).
   "Beige only" hides the camera picture (plain beige + hand outlines). Tracking keeps working.
   - Drum: right hand jab high = snare, jab low = kick. Left hand: hold at far-left edge = hat roll
     (FL tempo, 1/8 or 1/16), jab inside = perc/clap, jab up = open hat. Click pads with the mouse too.
   - Keyboard: hands FLAT, palms down, like hovering over piano keys.
     Angle the camera down and hold still briefly to learn your resting fingers.
     Press one finger DOWN toward the desk = key on, lift = off.
     Avoid a completely overhead/edge-on view where a dip cannot be seen. Slide the whole hand to shift notes.
     Keys light independently; whole-hand drops are ignored (start with 1-2 fingers).
   - Lead: move to a note and LAND (pause briefly) to select it, skipping passed notes.
      right hand left/right = notes in key, shake = vibrato, lift = bend to next note.
     Left hand up/down = volume, quick dip = retrigger. Needs both hands.
   - Strings: make a string: touch both index fingertips together (a dotted line appears), pull them
     apart to set its length and angle, then HOLD STILL a moment - it turns solid and colored. Move
     your hands away; it stays. Up to 8 strings.
     Play: swipe any fingertip through a string = pluck (faster = louder). Sweep a whole hand = strum.
     Notes: each new string takes the next note of the Key/Scale (e.g. E minor: E, F#, G, A, ...).
     "Length sets pitch" ON = longer string plays lower (still in key). Changing the key retunes them.
     Undo string / Clear strings buttons. Strings are saved with your FL project.
     Mouse: click a string = pluck, drag across = strum, Shift-drag (or right-drag) = draw a new string.
     The built-in pluck rings out on its own; MIDI notes last 0.6 s so FL synths get a clean note.
   Hand out of frame = that hand stops. The middle of the picture (your face) never plays
   (in Strings only your fingertips can pluck, so strings may cross the middle).
   For tighter feel set FL's audio buffer low (Options > Audio settings, e.g. 128-256 samples).

4) HEAR SERUM (or any synth) LIVE WHILE YOU PLAY
   Use ONE of these routes (not both, or you get double notes):

   A) Plugin MIDI out (simplest, inside FL)
      a. On Serum's channel: open its wrapper settings (gear icon) > MIDI input port = 1.
      b. On TENRINGS' wrapper settings (gear icon) > MIDI output port = 1.
      c. In TENRINGS turn "Built-in sound" OFF so you only hear Serum. Keep "Send MIDI" ON.
      Now your hands play Serum live. Lead sends pitch bend + CC11 (expression):
      set Serum's pitch-bend range to 2 semitones (3 if Easy 5-note + Minor is on) and map CC11 if you want the volume.

   B) Virtual MIDI port "TENRINGS" (FL sees your hands like a USB keyboard)
      a. In TENRINGS turn "Virtual port" ON (and "Send MIDI" OFF to avoid doubles).
      b. FL: Options > MIDI settings > Input list: enable "TENRINGS".
      c. Select the Serum channel in the Channel Rack - it plays like a keyboard. TENRINGS does not
         echo incoming MIDI back out, so there is no feedback loop even if it also receives it.

5) RECORD NOTES TO THE PIANO ROLL
   - Route B (virtual port) records exactly like a hardware keyboard: select the Serum (or TENRINGS)
     channel, click Record, choose "Notes" (or "Notes and automation"), play. Notes land in the piano roll.
   - Route A: FL records notes arriving at the Serum channel from MIDI port 1 the same way on most
     setups. If yours doesn't, use route B - it is the reliable keyboard-style path.
   - Afterwards slide the whole clip a little earlier for camera delay, or quantize.
   - Playback: notes in a pattern on the TENRINGS channel play its built-in sounds. Turn "Camera" OFF
     during playback so your hands don't double the recorded part.

Nothing is uploaded. The camera picture stays on your Mac.
